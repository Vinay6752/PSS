What we do

Security shaped
around your site.

Every location has different entry points, routines and risks. We help identify what matters, then plan the right mix of people and procedures.

01 / PEOPLE

Manned guarding

Uniformed guards for gate duty, patrolling and emergency response, supported by shift supervision and daily duty reporting.

02 / SPECIALIST

Ex-servicemen guards

Service-background personnel for sites requiring a higher level of protection. Armed deployment is subject to the required licences.

03 / SYSTEMS

Access control & alarms

Biometric and card entry, visitor registers, intrusion detection and fire alarm panels linked to control-room procedures.

04 / EVENTS

Event & VIP security

Entry checks, crowd-flow planning, bouncers, women guards where needed, VIP protection and parking coordination.

05 / RESPONSE

Mobile patrol & control room

Patrol rounds, GPS-tracked vehicles, guard-check calls and a control room number answered around the clock.
