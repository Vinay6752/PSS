What we do

Security shaped around your site.

Every location has different entry points, routines and risks. We help identify what matters, then plan the right mix of people, systems and procedures.

01 / PEOPLE

Manned guarding

Uniformed guards for gate duties, patrolling and site presence, supported by shift supervision and duty reporting.

02 / SPECIALIST

Ex-servicemen guards

Service-background personnel for sites that require a higher level of protection. Armed deployment is subject to applicable licences.

03 / SYSTEMS

Access control & alarms

Entry management, visitor records and alarm-system coordination supported by clear response procedures.

04 / EVENTS

Event & VIP security

Entry checks, crowd-flow coordination, bouncers, women guards where needed and parking management.

05 / RESPONSE

Mobile patrol & control room

Patrol rounds, guard-check calls and a control room number answered around the clock.
